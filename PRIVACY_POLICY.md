# Privacy Policy for BuildCalc

**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

**BuildCalc** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and information collection.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
BuildCalc is built from the ground up as a **100% offline, local jobsite instrument**:
* **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**
* **We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or payment details.**
* **We do not use tracking cookies, device fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers.**

---

### 2. Local Device Storage
All calculation records, project notes, stair profiles, rafter specifications, and bookmarked trade logs remain **strictly on your personal device**:
* Data is stored locally via your device's private application storage (`SharedPreferences`).
* Your data is never synced to external servers or accessible by third parties.
* You can clear your calculation history at any time directly within the app or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Network Access
* BuildCalc operates without network connectivity.
* The app contains **zero third-party advertising SDKs**, **zero analytics trackers** (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), and **zero crash reporters**.
* The app does not transmit any information over the internet.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
BuildCalc is a professional tool designed for construction tradespeople, contractors, framers, and estimators aged 18 and older. It does not solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we make any updates to this policy in future versions of the application, changes will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
