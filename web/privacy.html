Privacy Policy for BuildCalc

Last Updated: September 2026

BuildCalc ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices.

✓ 100% Offline & Private

BuildCalc does not collect, store, or transmit any user data over the internet. Everything runs strictly on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

BuildCalc operates as an offline digital trade instrument. We do not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, physical address).
  • Financial or payment details (handled entirely by Google Play).
  • Precise or approximate location data.
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising IDs.

2. Local Device Storage Only

Your saved calculations, bookmarks, and project notes are stored strictly in your device's private application sandbox (using standard local key-value storage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database. You can erase your history at any time from within the app.

3. No Ads & No Third-Party Tracking

BuildCalc contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero analytics or tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). The app makes zero external network calls.

4. Children's Privacy

BuildCalc is designed for professional contractors, tradespeople, and estimators aged 18 and older. It does not solicit or collect data from children under 13.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at vksolution.tech@gmail.com or through our developer profile on Google Play.
